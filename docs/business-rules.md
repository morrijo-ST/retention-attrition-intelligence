# Business Rules — Retention & Attrition Intelligence

**BR-001 — Starting denominator**  
Retention metrics use a governed starting recurring-revenue population for the selected period.

**BR-002 — Logo attrition**  
A logo is counted as lost only when the customer has no remaining qualifying recurring revenue under the defined scope.

**BR-003 — Revenue attrition**  
Lost recurring revenue is measured against the starting recurring-revenue base.

**BR-004 — GRR**  
Gross Revenue Retention excludes expansion and reflects only retained recurring revenue after churn and contraction.

**BR-005 — NRR**  
Net Revenue Retention includes expansion and contraction while remaining tied to the same starting recurring-revenue base.

**BR-006 — Expansion separation**  
Expansion revenue is reported separately so strong upsell performance does not hide gross retention weakness.

**BR-007 — Contraction classification**  
A customer that renews below the prior recurring value is classified as contraction rather than full retention.

**BR-008 — GACV movement**  
GACV growth and loss measures use governed renewal movement logic and remain reconcilable to underlying contracts.

**BR-009 — Health score transparency**  
Weighted health scores must document the component metrics and weights used.

**BR-010 — Commentary grounding**  
Executive commentary must be based on calculated measures and known segment drivers, not unsupported narrative inference.
