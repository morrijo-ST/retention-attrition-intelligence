# Case Study — Retention & Attrition Intelligence

## Business Problem
Recurring-revenue organizations often track churn at too high a level. A single attrition percentage does not explain whether losses come from logo churn, contraction, product mix, geography, or renewal behavior—and it does not show whether expansion offsets those losses.

## Objective
Create a governed retention intelligence framework that converts customer and contract movements into explainable revenue-health metrics for FP&A and executive decision-making.

## Solution Pattern
1. Establish a starting recurring-revenue baseline.
2. Classify renewal outcomes and revenue movements.
3. Separate churn, contraction, expansion, and flat renewals.
4. Calculate GRR, NRR, logo attrition, and revenue attrition.
5. Add GACV movement and coverage ratios.
6. Score overall revenue health using weighted metrics.
7. Translate results into management-ready commentary.

## Key Capabilities
- logo attrition
- revenue attrition
- GRR / NRR
- contraction and expansion
- lost recurring revenue
- GACV loss and growth
- coverage ratios
- weighted health scoring
- portfolio / region / product analysis
- executive commentary

## Public Portfolio Scope
The public version uses synthetic customer cohorts, contracts, renewal dates, and movement scenarios to demonstrate the methodology without exposing proprietary customer or revenue data.
