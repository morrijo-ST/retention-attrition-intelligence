# Metric Definitions — Retention & Attrition Intelligence

## Logo Attrition Rate
Percentage of starting customer logos that meet the governed loss definition during the selected period.

## Revenue Attrition Rate
Recurring revenue lost during the period divided by starting recurring revenue.

## Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)
Retention after churn and contraction, excluding expansion.

## Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
Retention after churn and contraction, including expansion.

## Lost Revenue
Recurring revenue removed through governed loss / non-renewal outcomes.

## Contraction Revenue
Recurring revenue reduction for customers that remain active at a lower recurring value.

## Expansion Revenue
Incremental recurring revenue above the prior recurring value for retained customers.

## Lost GACV
Growth-ACV value associated with customers or contracts classified as lost under the governed movement logic.

## Expansion Coverage Ratio
Positive expansion divided by loss / contraction exposure under the configured methodology.

## Growth Coverage Ratio
Total positive recurring growth compared with the negative recurring movement it is intended to offset.

## Overall Attrition Score
A weighted composite of selected attrition / retention measures used for management comparison.

## Overall Health Grade
A letter-grade interpretation of the composite health score using documented score bands.

> Thresholds and sample values in the public portfolio are synthetic/generalized.
