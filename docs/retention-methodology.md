# Retention Methodology — Retention & Attrition Intelligence

## Starting Point
All retention metrics begin with a governed starting recurring-revenue population for a defined period.

## Movement Classification
Each customer / contract movement is classified into one primary outcome:
- full renewal
- churn / non-renewal
- contraction
- expansion
- flat / no material change

## Core Formulas
### Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)
Starting recurring revenue less churn and contraction, divided by starting recurring revenue. Expansion is excluded.

### Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
Starting recurring revenue less churn and contraction plus expansion, divided by starting recurring revenue.

### Revenue Attrition Rate
Recurring revenue lost through churn and other governed loss categories divided by starting recurring revenue.

### Logo Attrition Rate
Lost customer logos divided by starting customer logos, subject to the governed logo-loss definition.

## Coverage Ratios
Expansion or growth coverage compares positive recurring-revenue movement to lost / contracted recurring value to show whether growth offsets attrition.

## Health Scoring
Selected retention and growth metrics are normalized, weighted, and converted into an overall score and management grade. Weights are documented and reproducible.

## Reconciliation
Starting revenue + expansion - contraction - churn should reconcile to ending recurring revenue after documented adjustments.

> Public examples are synthetic and metric thresholds are generalized.
