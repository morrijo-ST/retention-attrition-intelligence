# Operations Runbook — Retention & Attrition Intelligence

## Refresh Sequence
1. Confirm contract and renewal source data is complete.
2. Establish the governed starting recurring-revenue population.
3. Validate customer / product / region mappings.
4. Classify renewal movements.
5. Calculate churn, contraction, expansion, GRR, NRR, and attrition metrics.
6. Reconcile movement totals back to starting and ending recurring revenue.
7. Calculate health scores / grades.
8. Review material drivers before publishing commentary.

## Common Failures
### Denominator mismatch
Confirm the selected starting population and fiscal period before calculating retention.

### Movement does not reconcile
Review multi-contract customers, partial renewals, late renewals, and classification overlaps.

### GRR / NRR discrepancy
Confirm expansion is excluded from GRR and included correctly in NRR.

### Unexpected logo loss
Check whether the customer retains qualifying recurring revenue on another contract or product.

## Publication Control
Headline retention and attrition measures should reconcile to the underlying movement population before executive reporting is distributed.
